NUTRITION

Ingredient transparency without fake health claims.

Final nutrition values depend on verified recipes, portions and suppliers. This page explains how the production site should present that information.

Recipe-level data

Publish calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat and sugars only after recipes and serving sizes are finalized.

Customizations

Boosters, substitutions and toppings change nutrition values. The menu should make that clear.

Not medical advice

Menu descriptions should not promise treatment, weight loss or performance outcomes.

Ask about ingredients

Customers should have an easy way to confirm current ingredients and brand substitutions.
