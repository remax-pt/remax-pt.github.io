FAQ

Useful answers, not generic booking questions.

Can I customize drinks?

Most made-to-order smoothies and shakes can be adjusted with available bases, ingredients and boosters.

Do you offer dairy-free options?

Several recipes use oat, almond or coconut-based ingredients, but actual ingredient labels and cross-contact procedures must be verified.

Can I pre-order?

This prototype includes a local demo builder. Production should connect to a real POS or ordering provider.

Do you cater?

Yes, the prototype includes bottled smoothie, breakfast and group order concepts.

Are nutrition values published?

Only after final recipes, portions and suppliers are verified.

Do you handle allergies?

Shared equipment creates cross-contact risk. Review the allergen page and speak with staff before ordering.
